Health Connect permissions

What Vaylo Sports reads, why, and how you stay in control.

What we read

Vaylo Sports asks Health Connect for read-only access, and only to the six types of record below. We never write to Health Connect, and we do not request access to anything else.

Record typeWhy
Exercise sessionsTo show your completed sessions and the training you have actually done
DistanceTo measure running, cycling and swimming volume
Total calories burnedTo estimate training load and energy use
Heart rateTo show intensity and help keep hard days hard and easy days easy
StepsTo include everyday movement alongside structured training
SleepTo factor recovery into your training load

What happens to it

  • It is stored in your Vaylo Sports account so your training, load and recovery views reflect what you have actually done.
  • It is never used for advertising, and it is never sold.
  • Sponsorship partners receive only aggregate counts of how many times a campaign was shown or tapped, per campaign per day. No personal data, and nothing derived from your health records, is shared with them.
  • You can delete the data, and your whole account, at any time — see the links below.

How to change your mind

Open the Health Connect app (or Settings → Security and privacy → Privacy controls → Health Connect), choose Apps, then Vaylo Sports, and remove any or all of the permissions. Revoking access stops any further syncing immediately; data already synced stays until you delete it.

Kept on your device

Health data is used to build your training views and calculate load and recovery. It is never used for advertising and never sold. You can delete the synced data, or your whole account, from Profile → Settings in the app.

Our full privacy policy

The Vaylo Sports privacy policy covers the categories of data we handle, every processor that works on our behalf, how long we keep data, and your rights. It is linked from the app and from our Google Play listing.
