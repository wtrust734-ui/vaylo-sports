Vaylo Sports is an adult sports-performance platform, intended for 18–30-year-old athletes — from developing athletes through serious and competitive ones. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who else processes it, and how you get it back or get rid of it. It covers the Vaylo Sports Android app and any web version of it.

That age range describes who the app is built for. It is not a technical age gate: we do not verify anyone's age, we do not check identity documents, and we do not block anyone by age, so the app can be used by an athlete under 18. If you are under 18, section 7 sets out exactly what changes for you — and those protections are enforced, not advisory.

The short version. We collect what the app needs to coach you: your account details, the training you log, health and fitness data you choose to connect, and the content you post. Two things are worth calling out because they involve sending data to someone else — AI features send the relevant text or video to OpenAI or Google to generate a response, and a connected wearable's data is read from that provider with your permission. We do not sell your data, we do not use health data for advertising, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

1. What we collect

Account details

Your email address and password (we never see the password — it is hashed by our authentication provider). During onboarding we ask for your sport, experience level, training goals and training days, and we ask on a separate step for your height and weight. Optionally: your name, date of birth and country. Height and weight are optional and can be left blank; when you give them they are stored on your profile and used only to size training loads and to show trends to you. They are never shown on your public profile, never used for advertising, and never sold. You can change or remove them at any time in Profile. If you sign in with a passkey, we store the public key credential, never a biometric — your fingerprint or face never leaves your device.

Your date of birth, if you give it. It is optional, and it is the field that decides two things: the age-based training limits described in section 7, and whether sponsored content may be shown to you, which is adults only. If you leave it blank we cannot apply age-based adjustments and no sponsored content is shown. It is also the only age information we hold: the app runs no age verification and no identity checks, and we do not ask for anyone else's details to check yours.

Guardian and family access

A guardian link is opt-in and belongs to the athlete. An athlete creates a short single-use code in the app; whoever redeems it becomes the linked guardian, and the link stays pending until the athlete approves it. Where an account is held for a child under 13, the link is made immediately instead, because in that case the adult is the person responsible for the account. The record holds the two account ids, the relationship the guardian chose (for example parent or carer), the permissions the athlete granted, the link status, and the invite code's issue and expiry times. A linked guardian sees a reduced summary of that athlete's training — load and recovery bands, and safety alerts — never the training log itself, and the athlete can change the permissions or revoke the link at any time.

Support and legal requests

When you use the in-app support or contact form, and when you email us, we keep what you send: a reference number, the category you chose, the contact address you gave us, an optional account reference and display name, your subject and message, and a one-way hash of the network address plus the user-agent of the request, which is used only to investigate abuse. This is a correspondence record rather than account content, so it is not part of the in-app data download and is not erased by deleting your account — sections 8 and 11 explain how to have it removed.

Training and activity

Sessions you log or track, plus everything derived from them: training load, personal bests, streaks, points, achievements, readiness and recovery scores. Workouts you track with GPS include the route and its start and end points.

Content you create

Posts, comments, challenges, community messages, direct messages, your profile photo and avatar, and anything you share publicly. Public content is visible to other athletes — that is the point of it — so treat it as public.

Purchases

Your coin and credit balance, what you bought, and subscription status. Payment for Android purchases is handled entirely by Google Play; we receive a purchase token and a receipt, never your card number, and we do not store your billing address.

Waiting list

If you join the Vaylo Sports waiting list on our website, we store the email address you give us, your name if you choose to provide one, the date and time you joined, a note of where the signup came from, and a short salted one-way hash of the network address the request came from. That hash is used only to stop one source signing up hundreds of times; it is not readable and is not used to identify you. We use the address to send a confirmation when you join, a short run of updates in the weeks before the Android release opens, and the launch-day message. There is no newsletter and no other mailing. Every email carries a link that removes you from the list immediately.

Technical data

Standard server logs (IP address, request time, user agent, error details) that our hosting provider records to keep the service running and to stop abuse. We do not run third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, and there is no advertising identifier in this app.

If something in the app fails, the app itself sends us a short diagnostic report: the type of failure, its message, the screen it happened on, the app version and your device's browser or app string. We use them to fix the app. They contain no health or fitness data, no message contents, no photographs and no sign-in tokens, and anything that looks like a token, an email address or a long identifier is removed on your device before the report is sent. They are not linked to your account in any way, so we cannot pick out yours — there is nothing in them that identifies you. They are stored by the same hosting provider as the rest of the service, are never sold or shared, and are not used to build a profile about you.

2. Health and fitness data

Health data only comes from a source you explicitly connect. There is no background collection and no path where it arrives without your action.

SourceWhat we read
Health Connect (Android) Exercise sessions, distance, total calories burned, heart rate, steps and sleep. Read-only: the app never writes to Health Connect.
Connected wearable services
Strava, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Polar, Suunto, COROS and others		 Activities, workouts and, where the provider offers it, recovery and sleep data. You authorise each one through that provider's own consent screen and can revoke it there at any time.
Your device sensors While you have a workout actively tracking, the app reads your device location to measure how far you have gone. It calculates the distance on your device and stores only the resulting distance — your coordinates and the route are never saved, and location is never read in the background. Your country for leaderboards is a value you pick, not something derived from location.
You, directly Anything you type or record: RPE, injuries, soreness, mood, nutrition, hydration.

Health and fitness data is used to build your training views, calculate load and recovery, and generate coaching output. It is not used for advertising or marketing and is never sold.

To revoke Health Connect access, open the Health Connect app (or Settings → Security and privacy → Privacy controls → Health Connect) and remove permissions for Vaylo Sports. Deleting a connection in the app stops future syncing but does not remove data already synced — see Deleting your account for that.

3. AI features and what leaves the app

Coaching features are generated by large language models. When you use one, the text you submit and the minimum context needed to answer it is sent to the provider below. This is the only time your content leaves our own systems.

FeatureWhat is sentProcessed by
Coach chat, training plans, weekly review, learning recommendations Your message plus a summary of your profile and recent training (sport, level, goals, logged sessions, injuries you recorded) so the advice fits you. OpenAI
Video form analysis You upload a video, which is sent in full (up to roughly 18 MB) along with your sport and relevant profile context. Google (Gemini API)

Do not put information into these features that you are not willing to have processed this way. Both providers are used under their API terms rather than their consumer terms, and we grant them no permission to use your content for advertising.

What we do not claim about AI retention. What each provider retains after a request, and whether a request is used to improve its models, is governed by that provider's own API terms. Our requests do not set a zero-retention or no-training option, and this policy therefore makes no promise that AI input is never stored or never used for model improvement. Requests are processed on infrastructure that may sit outside your country — see section 13.

4. Why we use it, and our legal bases

  • To run the service you asked for — your account, your training data, your coaching output. Legal basis: performing our contract with you.
  • To process health and fitness data — with your explicit consent, which you give when you connect Health Connect or a wearable and can withdraw at any time. Legal basis: explicit consent.
  • To keep the service safe and working — abuse prevention, rate limiting, fraud and refund handling, fixing crashes. Legal basis: our legitimate interests, and our legal obligations where purchases are involved.
  • To tell you about the app — service emails such as sign-in and security notices, and notifications you opt into. Legal basis: the contract, or your consent for optional notifications.
  • To tell you when the Android release opens — if you joined the waiting list, we use the address you gave for a confirmation when you join, for the short run of updates before the release opens, and for the launch-day message, and for nothing else. Legal basis: your consent, given when you join and withdrawable at any time with the link in any email we send.

We do not profile you for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions with legal effects about you.

7. Age, and if you are under 18

Who the app is for. Vaylo Sports is intended for 18–30-year-old athletes, from developing athletes through serious and competitive ones. That is the market the app is designed and written for, and it is not a technical restriction: we do not verify age or identity, and we do not block anyone by age. Our Terms set the minimum age for holding an account at 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13.

If you are under 18, these apply. Features are switched off or limited for any account whose date of birth shows it is under 18, and the limits are enforced in the database or in the app's own code rather than hidden in a screen:

  • No sponsored content or advertising anywhere in the app, and none of your data is counted in any aggregate a brand sees. The sponsor database itself enforces an 18+ minimum.
  • Your profile is private and cannot be made public, enforced by a database trigger rather than a setting you could be talked out of.
  • No nutrition, diet or calorie-target features. We do not estimate calories or macros for an under-18 account, and we do not build diet plans for one.
  • Training volume is capped by age — fewer sessions, shorter sessions, more rest days — and the caps apply to what the app suggests and to what it flags.
  • No promotional nudges: no discount offers, no bonus-credit pushes, no "picked for you" recommendations.
  • A guardian can be linked to the account with the athlete's approval, as section 1 describes, and any purchase made on an under-18 account is refunded in full when it is called up.

What we do not claim. We are not certified under, and do not claim certification against, the ICO's Children's Code, COPPA, or any other certification scheme. The controls above are age-appropriate design choices we have actually implemented; the full description, including what we have not finalised, is in Child & Young Athlete Safety. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's account at any time and without giving a reason.

One honest limitation: we rely on the date of birth an athlete gives us, and we have not implemented age assurance. We are not adding identity checks to solve this, because that would collect more personal data from every user than the problem warrants. Someone who enters a different date of birth receives the protections for that date of birth rather than for their real age. See Child & Young Athlete Safety for what that means in practice.

8. How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it — until you delete it.
  • Server logs — a short, rolling window measured in days to weeks, then discarded.
  • Purchase and refund records — kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, separate from your profile.
  • Support and legal correspondence — kept while we handle the request and afterwards so we can show how it was answered. It is not removed by deleting your account; ask us and we will delete it. We have not fixed a retention period for it yet, and we are not going to publish a number we have not chosen.
  • One deletion record — when you delete your account we keep a single row proving the deletion happened. It contains a salted hash of your user id, a status and a timestamp. No name, no email, nothing that identifies you.
One retention period we have not settled. Server logs can contain your IP address, the time of the request, your device's user-agent string and error details. "A short rolling window" describes the policy but is not a number you can hold us to, so we are not going to dress it up as one. The exact retention period for server logs has not been decided and is not published here. When it is fixed it will appear in this section, and this page will be updated with its version number at the same time.

9. Safety, reporting and blocking

The feed, communities, challenges and leaderboards contain content posted by other athletes, including athletes from other sports and countries. Every shared activity and profile carries a Report control. Choosing a reason and sending it writes a row containing your user id, what you reported, when, and any words you added. We use it only to review that content.

  • Only you can see the reports you have sent. Other athletes cannot see that you reported them, and cannot see your report.
  • We do not show the athlete you reported to us who reported them.
  • You can also block an athlete. Blocking removes their shared activity from your feed, stops them sending you friend requests, and is not disclosed to them.
  • If you delete your account, your reports are deleted with it.

11. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account permanently, or follow these steps.

Deletion is immediate and complete. We erase your profile, training history, health data, content, messages, purchases and uploaded files, and remove the login itself. The app then verifies the wipe by counting rows in every table that can hold your data and confirms nothing remains. You do not need to email us, and there is no waiting period.

Two records sit outside that: the single deletion record described in section 8, and any support or legal correspondence you have sent us, which is a separate record rather than account content. Email us if you want a correspondence record deleted too, and we will do it unless we are required to keep it for a legal claim.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Access to your records is enforced per-row in the database, not just in the app, so one athlete's data cannot be read by another. Passwords are stored hashed. Administrative access to production is restricted, and the server verifies every purchase with Google before granting anything.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

13. Where your data is stored

Our database and servers are hosted by Supabase, and AI requests are processed by OpenAI and Google. These providers operate infrastructure in several regions, including the United States, so your data may be processed outside the country you live in. Where data leaves the UK, EU or Switzerland, the transfer relies on the providers' standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards.
