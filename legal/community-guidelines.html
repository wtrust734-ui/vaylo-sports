Vaylo Sports has a public side. You can share what you have trained, join a community, enter a challenge and appear on a leaderboard. That only works if the people in it behave decently toward each other. These are the rules, they apply to everyone regardless of age or sporting level, and breaking them gets content removed and can get an account suspended.

Who these rules cover. They apply to everything you post or send that another person can see or hear: shared activities, your profile and bio, community posts and comments, challenge entries, leaderboard names, direct messages, and anything you put in a note or a story.

1. Be decent

  • Talk to people as you would want to be talked to. This includes people who are newer or less experienced than you. Being good at something is not a licence to make someone feel small.
  • No bullying, harassment or threats — no piling on, no dogpiling, no "killing" someone's session, and no threats of any kind, including joking ones aimed at someone who is not in on the joke.
  • No hate. Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic or any other content attacking people for who they are is removed, not debated.
  • No sexual content, and absolutely no sexualising a child or an adult who looks like a child. If in doubt, do not post it.
  • Do not impersonate. Do not pose as Vaylo Sports, as one of our staff, as a coach, or as another athlete.
  • Mark your AI-assisted content as AI-assisted if we ask you to. If you paste generated training advice into the community as though a human wrote it, that is a breach of these rules.
  • Play by the rules of the sport. Cheating, doping, and faking a result will get you removed, and we will pass relevant details to the relevant body where we are obliged to.

2. Never acceptable

The following are removed. Depending on severity and whether it is a first offence, the account that posted them is suspended:

  • Harassment, bullying or threats directed at anyone.
  • Racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic or otherwise hateful content.
  • Sexual content, or any sexualisation of a child.
  • Content encouraging self-harm, disordered eating, or dangerous training.
  • Posting someone else's personal information without their consent.
  • Impersonating Vaylo Sports, a coach, or another athlete.

3. Training, health and eating

This is the part of the community we watch most closely, because a young or desperate athlete — of any age — will believe a stranger.

  • Do not give medical, diagnostic or injury advice to another athlete. Do not tell someone what injury they have, or how to rehab it.
  • Do not promote restrictive eating, fasting, purging, or weight targets — especially not to an under-18 athlete or anyone who has told you they are training hard.
  • Do not encourage dangerous training: training through sharp pain, training while injured, escalating load far beyond what someone has done before, or using performance products in ways that are not safe.
  • Do not shame anyone for resting. Fatigue and injury are not character flaws, and a culture that treats them as one causes real harm.
  • Promoting self-harm, suicide or anything that glorifies an eating disorder is an instant removal and, where the law allows it, a report to the relevant authority.
Being encouraging is the point. Saying "that session looked brutal, how are the legs?" is good. Diagnosing them, prescribing them a plan, or telling them to push through pain is not, even if you are right.

4. Other people's information

  • Do not post someone else's personal data — not their address, school, team, phone number, social handles, or an image of them. This includes images where they are simply in the background.
  • Do not dox or pile on. Coordinating a group to target one person is harassment, and it is treated as harassment.
  • Do not share screenshots of conversations with Vaylo staff or other athletes without consent.
  • Get consent before you post someone. A photo of them training is still a photo of them.

If someone posts your information without your consent, report it. That is one of the report reasons the app gives you, and we remove it.

5. Advertising and spam

  • No unsolicited selling in the community. You cannot use Vaylo Sports to advertise a supplement, a program, a gym, a coaching service or an app.
  • No repeated identical posts, no fake accounts, no automated posting.
  • No scams, phishing, or links to anything that asks for your password or payment details.
  • Paid partnerships are labelled. Any sponsor content that appears without a label is a bug — please report it.

Sponsors do not get to see who looked at their placement, and cannot contact you through the app. See the Advertising & Tracking Disclosure.

6. If you are under 18

The rules are the same, and in a few areas they are stricter because the risk is higher.

  • No sponsorship content is shown to you at all.
  • Your profile is private by default and cannot be made public, so your shared activity is not broadcast.
  • Nutrition and weight-related features are switched off for you. You will see an explanation rather than a silent block.
  • More reports are reviewed as a priority, and content targeting children is treated as the most serious category there is.
  • Adults must not contact you directly. If someone does, block them and report it.

More detail is in Child & Young Athlete Safety.

What happens to your report

  • A person reads it. Reports are not handled by an automated filter deciding whether you were right.
  • Only you can see the reports you have sent. Another athlete cannot see that you reported them, and we do not tell the athlete you reported who reported them.
  • Reporting twice does not stack. If you have already reported the same thing, the app tells you so rather than pretending it is new.
  • Your report is deleted with your account.
Urgent safety reports. If someone is in immediate danger, or there is content involving the sexual exploitation of a child, please also report it to the police or to the NSPCC on the Child Protection Helpline. Email us as well so we can act on the account, but do not rely on our inbox as the route to emergency help.

8. Blocking someone

You can block any athlete from their profile. Blocking:

  • removes their shared activity from your feed, in both directions;
  • stops them sending you friend requests;
  • is not disclosed to them — they are not told they were blocked.

Blocking is a personal boundary, not a moderation tool. It does not remove the content from the app or from anyone else's feed, and it is not a way to escalate a dispute — if you want the content gone, report it as well.

9. What we do when rules are broken

We take enforcement in proportion to what happened. In escalating order:

  • Remove the content and leave everything else alone.
  • Remove the content and issue a warning, recorded against the account.
  • Suspend the account temporarily, which removes its content and blocks it from the community features for a defined period.
  • Close the account permanently. Used for the most serious cases and for repeat offences.
  • Report to a third party where the law requires or permits it — for example child sexual exploitation material, or a credible threat of violence. We do this whether or not you asked us to.

11. Acceptable use, summarised

This page is the Acceptable Use Policy for Vaylo Sports. If you only read one thing, read this section.

Use the app to log your own training, understand it, and share what you want to share. That is what it is for, and almost everything here is about keeping it that way.

Do not use it to:

  • harass, threaten, bully, or target anyone — in the app or anywhere else using an account here;
  • post sexual content, or sexualise anyone, and especially not a minor;
  • post or promote self-harm, disordered eating, or dangerous training;
  • hate anyone — racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise;
  • impersonate Vaylo Sports, a coach, a creator, or another athlete;
  • post someone else's private information, images or messages without their consent;
  • advertise, spam, scam, or run a scheme through the app;
  • scrape, copy, reverse engineer, or attack the app, its API, or other people's accounts;
  • use it to contact, groom, or identify a child, or to find out where one is;
  • mislead others about who you are, or claim to be a doctor, physiotherapist or coach when you are not.

Automated access is not permitted. Do not scrape, crawl, or use bots or scripts against the app, the website or the API, and do not attempt to breach, overload or gain unauthorised access to anything.

Breaking these rules can lead to content being removed, to limits on an account, or to an account being closed. Reporting a concern about someone else is never a breach of these rules — see Report Content or a User. If you think we enforced this wrongly, see the complaints procedure.
