1. The cookies we set

This is the complete list. It has no entries.

This website sets no cookie, the app sets no cookie, and the downloadable web-app clone sets no cookie. No analytics cookie, no advertising cookie, no session cookie, and no cookie that records whether you accepted something — not even this notice.

It is worth being concrete about why, because "we only use essential cookies" is a sentence usually written by companies using everything else. This site is a set of static pages: the stylesheet, the small script that runs the menu and the forms, and the images are all served from this same domain, and not one of them writes a cookie. The app is an Android application, and it keeps its preferences in your device's own storage rather than in cookies — the complete list is in section 4 below. The only way a cookie could reach us is if you sign in with Google, Facebook or Discord, which sets cookies on their domain while you are there, not on ours.

Whether you acknowledge the notice at the foot of the website is remembered on your device, not in a cookie and not on our servers. Section 5 explains exactly what that means.

2. What we do not use

To be specific, because "we only use essential cookies" is often written by companies using everything else:

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog or equivalent.
  • No advertising or ad-personalisation cookies, and no advertising identifier.
  • No social media pixels or tracking pixels, including in the legal pages themselves.
  • No session recording, heatmaps, A/B testing or personalisation tools from third parties.
  • No embedded third-party content that would set a cookie — each legal document is a self-contained page with no scripts, no fonts, no images and no iframes of its own, and the website around it loads nothing from any other domain.
  • No fingerprinting, no canvas or font probing, and no device fingerprint hash.

Signing in with Google, Facebook or Discord takes you to that provider to confirm your identity. They may set their own cookies on their domain while you are there. We do not control those and we never see them. Passkey sign-in is entirely local to your device.

5. The notice you saw, and what Accept does

The Vaylo Sports website shows a small notice at the bottom of the page when you first visit it. It says that we set no tracking cookies and links to this document. Pressing Accept does one thing: it writes a single entry in your browser's own storage, on your device, recording that you have read the notice. The app itself shows no cookie notice, because it has no cookie for you to be told about — the settings it keeps are the list in section 4, and they are part of the app rather than something a browser sends us.

  • It is not a consent record. Nothing on this site needs your consent, because nothing here is non-essential. There is no cookie to switch on or off, and no feature is withheld until you press the button.
  • Nothing is sent to us. The acknowledgement stays in your browser. We cannot see it, count it, or tie it to you, and no server of ours receives it.
  • It does not gate anything. Every page, form and link works whether you press Accept, ignore it, or never see it because your browser blocks scripts.
  • It can be forgotten. Clearing your browser's site data brings the notice back, and showing it again is the correct behaviour rather than a bug.

If we ever add something that genuinely needs consent — a non-essential cookie, an analytics tool, an embedded video — that will be a real consent choice: off until you turn it on, and changeable afterwards, not an "accept all" on a notice about essential storage. Nothing currently on this site or in the app is in that category.

6. How to control it

  • In the app: clearing the app's storage, or deleting the app, removes all of it.
  • On this website and in the downloadable web-app clone: clearing cookies and site data for this site removes the cookie notice acknowledgement, so the notice will appear again. Nothing else changes.
  • In your browser: every major browser lets you delete cookies and site data for a site. Doing that removes the settings listed above, and costs you nothing but your preferences.
  • If your browser blocks storage entirely, the site and app still work — they just forget your preferences between sessions, and the notice is shown every time.
