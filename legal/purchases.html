1. What credits and coins are

Vaylo Sports has three kinds of in-app item:

  • Credits buy access to individual AI features and analysis — a coach message, a generated training week, a form analysis. New accounts start with 260 credits at no cost, which is enough to build a full 8-week training plan and still use the app a little.
  • Coins are earned through activity and spent on cosmetic items. They have no effect on features.
  • Event Packs give access to a specific event or programme for the period stated on its page. They are one-off, not a subscription.

2. They are not money

Credits, coins and Event Packs are virtual items with no cash value. Specifically:

  • They are not currency and cannot be redeemed for cash or anything of value.
  • They cannot be sold, traded, gifted, transferred or bequeathed, and there is no facility to withdraw them.
  • They are a licence to use features inside Vaylo Sports and nothing more.
  • They do not expire while your account is open, but they are lost if you delete your account — and deletion cannot be undone.
  • They may be removed if a purchase they came from is refunded, charged back, reversed, or found to be fraudulent.
The honest caveat on this section. Whether a court would enforce a "no cash value" term against a consumer depends on the law of the country they live in, and in some jurisdictions such a term is not effective for digital content once you have begun to use it. We are telling you the position we intend to operate, not promising a legal outcome. If you disagree with it, your statutory rights remain — see section 10.

We would rather charge for the calls we make than treat one payment as covering a year of them.

Money prices are shown at checkout and nowhere else. AI-generated features are not currently live in the app, and no AI feature is charged in this build. 4. How you earn them

You get credits without paying for:

  • 260 at sign-up.
  • A daily reward for consecutive days, on a ladder that increases through the week.
  • Rewards for referring a friend, for both of you: 50 credits for the referrer and 25 for the friend who joins.
  • Rewards for certain activities in the app.
  • Any promotion we choose to run, stated in the app when it is running.

5. How buying works

  • Payments are taken by Google Play on Android, using Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card details.
  • Coach membership is the exception: it is sold on our website, paid by card through Stripe, and never sold inside the app. We receive a payment reference from Stripe, never your card number.
  • Google's payment terms and refund policy apply to the transaction itself, alongside your rights under our terms.
  • We verify every purchase with Google before granting anything. The app cannot grant credits on its own.
  • Currency and price are shown at checkout and may differ by country. You are charged the amount displayed before you confirm.
  • You will be charged as an adult purchasing for yourself. See section 8.

6. Subscriptions

If a subscription is offered, it renews automatically at the interval shown at checkout, at the price displayed there, until you cancel. Unlimited access, where offered, removes the per-use credit cost for the features it covers, subject to a daily fair-use ceiling on AI calls that is enforced automatically; the current ceiling is shown in the app.

  • Cancel in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and lets the current paid period run out.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, and deleting your Vaylo account does not cancel it either. Cancel with Google separately.
  • Unused purchased time is not refunded on cancellation, except where the law gives you a right to a refund.
  • We may change subscription pricing. We will tell you before a change affects you, and you can cancel first.
  • Very long or one-off "lifetime" purchases, where offered, are not subscriptions and do not renew.

7. Spending and credit loss

  • Spending is immediate and final once a feature runs.
  • Nothing is reserved — credits are taken when you confirm, and if the feature then fails to run, tell us and we will refund the credits.
  • Unused credits roll over between months on a subscription, up to 300 credits, rather than expiring.
  • If we shut a feature down, credits already spent on it are not refunded, but any locked unlock stays unlocked.
  • Deleting your account loses everything. Ask for an export first — see Deleting Your Account.

8. Under-18s, buying and refunds

Checkout is not switched on, so nobody of any age can buy anything in the app today. What follows is our position for when it is, and the part of it that applies whatever happens in the meantime.

A purchase made by someone under 18 must involve a parent or guardian. Beyond that, we do not ask an under-18 athlete to justify themselves: any purchase made on an account registered to someone under 18 is refunded in full on request — from the athlete or from a parent or guardian — with no reason required. That commitment does not depend on whether the credits were spent, on how long ago the purchase was beyond what Google Play's process allows, or on anything else in this document. See Refunds and Cancellations.

The buying controls are available to an under-18 account, and an under-18 account is shown a note saying exactly what is above: that a purchase should involve a parent or guardian, and that it is refunded in full on request. What is not offered to an under-18 account is the promotion — no discount banner, no bonus-credit push and no product recommendation. A price on a shop page is information; a countdown and a bonus are persuasion, and those stay adult-only. Coins earned through activity are unaffected. See Child & Young Athlete Safety.

9. If we change prices or things

We may change credit costs, pack sizes or prices. Where a change would cost you more:

  • we will tell you in the app before it applies to you, and you can buy more at the old price first;
  • we do not retroactively reprice credits you already hold;
  • we do not remove a feature you have already paid to unlock from an account that has paid for it, unless we say why and give you something of equivalent value.

10. Your rights are not affected

Nothing in this document removes a right the law gives you. In particular, where you are a consumer:

  • you keep your statutory rights against faulty content or a service that is not of a reasonable standard, including the right to a refund where you have lost the benefit of something you paid for;
  • "no cash value" and "non-refundable" do not override those rights, and no clause here is intended to.
  • Nothing here is drafted to be unfair or to prevent you complaining or taking action. Terms that do that are unenforceable under UK consumer law regardless of what they say.

11. Virtual items: the terms that apply to them

Credits, coins, and anything else you can hold inside Vaylo Sports are virtual items. This section is the terms that apply to them, gathered in one place.

They are not money, and they are not property. They have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, transferred, sold, gifted or exchanged — including with another account, and including for anything of real value. They exist only inside this app.

You hold a licence, not an asset. We grant you a personal, non-transferable, revocable right to use virtual items while your account exists and you use them. Because of that, we can change what they cost, what they buy, or remove them altogether. We will not remove items you paid real money for without refunding them, but the general point stands: a virtual item is not a contractual benefit we owe you indefinitely.

They have no value outside the app. If a service stops, or we stop operating, that is a loss of a virtual item, not a loss of money. We do not owe you the cash value of anything you have not paid us.

No cashing out, no third-party markets. Selling or buying virtual items for real money outside the app is not something we permit, and doing so risks the account being closed. It also creates a route for laundering, so we do not want any part of it.

An under-18 cannot buy anything right now, because checkout is off for every age, and when it opens a purchase on an under-18 account will require a parent or guardian and will be refunded in full on request (section 8). See Child & Young Athlete Safety.

Where you have paid real money and something has gone wrong, your consumer rights are unaffected — see section 10 above and Refunds & Cancellation. Nothing in this section removes a right you have under the law of your country.
