About

The story of Vaylo Sports

A training log with analysis attached, built by one person over four years. You record what you train; the app works it back to you. That is the whole idea, and it is deliberately narrower than what most apps in this category promise.

01 2024 – 2027

Four years, one build.

The idea arrived in 2024. Building started in 2025. It has been built through 2026, and it launches in 2027.

Nothing about that timeline was planned as a marketing story. It is how long it took to get the parts that matter right, and to stop adding the parts that did not.

2024

The idea. The starting point was a gap: a training log already knows every session you have done, and still shows you a calendar. The idea for Vaylo was to make the record answer back — load, readiness, recovery, and the single limiter worth working on.

2025

Building begins. The first year of writing the app: a log that is quick to fill in, metrics that are arithmetic on that log rather than guesses, and a plan that adapts to the week you actually had. The all-sport decision was made here, because the underlying maths does not care which sport produced the effort.

2026Now

The long build. The year spent making the app safe to hand to someone: age caps and switched-off features for younger athletes, a refund and purchase path that works without emailing anyone, and twenty-six published documents behind every claim the product makes. Slower than adding features, and the reason it can launch at all.

2027

Launch. The Android release opens. Until then the app is not on the Play Store, and this site will not link to a listing that does not exist. The waiting list is the only place that changes.

Purpose

Why it exists

Most training apps collect what you do and then show you a streak. Vaylo was built around the opposite question: given what you actually logged, what does that actually say?

That produces a handful of tools that are more useful than a streak — a readiness number next to last night’s sleep, an acute load that flags a three-week ramp, a Limiter Test that tells you which single thing is most likely holding you back instead of telling you to do everything.

It also produces a responsibility. A number that looks like an insight will be believed, so the app has to say clearly where a number comes from, and has to stop rather than advise when the honest answer is “ask someone who can see you”.

Approach

Why it works across every sport

The core of Vaylo is sport-agnostic on purpose. Load, recovery, readiness and the limiter logic are built from effort and duration — things a rugby player, a 10k runner, a swimmer and a climber all have in common. The sport picker changes terminology and event context; it does not change the maths.

Most athletes train more than one thing. Cross-training is where Vaylo earns its keep: two sports competing for the same recovery capacity, or a second sport quietly fixing or quietly breaking a first.

Audience

Who it is designed for

It suits you if…

  • You train several times a week and want the log to do something rather than just hold it.
  • You are working towards an event and want a structure to follow.
  • You are returning from a break and want to see where you actually are.
  • You train in more than one sport and want to see the interaction.
  • You are 13 to 17 and want a tool that does not try to sell you anything.

It is not for you if…

  • You want a doctor, a diagnosis or a treatment. It is not one, and will not pretend.
  • You want a coach who knows you. It has no idea what happened today.
  • You are looking for something to replace clinical advice after an injury.
  • You are under 13. This app is not directed at children under 13.

Honesty

What Vaylo will not claim

  • Vaylo does not prevent injury. It has an injury-risk flag, which is arithmetic on your own data, not a medical assessment.
  • Vaylo does not guarantee performance. Nothing here promises you will get faster, stronger or healthier.
  • Vaylo has no medical approval of any kind. No regulator, medical body or standards organisation has reviewed or endorsed it, and there is no clinical validation behind any number in the app.
  • Vaylo holds no certification or accreditation. No sport body, governing organisation or standards body has endorsed it or reviewed this app.
  • Vaylo does not sell your data and does not share it for advertising.
