Before you write

Urgent and safety issues

If a child is at immediate risk, call the police or the NSPCC Child Protection Helpline on 0808 8000 111 — do not wait for us.

If you cannot sign in and the account is locked, email us and we will get you in. Do not keep trying, or the account can lock further.

If you have been charged and not received what you paid for, or a subscription does not cancel, tell us now. Refunds and cancellations are handled as soon as the charge is confirmed.

What happens next

What an email to us looks like

  • You send us an email — from the form below or by replying to a received reply.
  • We store it — in the support_requests inbox, which only the operator can see.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere else — we do not add you to a mailing list, and we have no marketing list.
  • We reply to the email address you give — on the same thread, so there is no guessing which account to find.
  • A reply address can end the stream — the one-click control at the bottom of our reply stops that address from receiving any further support email.

Send a message to the operator

Choose the category that best matches what you need, and tell us what happened. We will reply to the address you give, on the same thread so there is no guessing.
