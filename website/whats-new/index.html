What has changed, and when

Every change to this website and to the published documents, newest first. When a policy changes, the version it moved to is named here as well as on the document itself.

This is not app release notes. There is no public release yet, so there is nothing to write release notes about, and inventing a history for a product that has not shipped would be the least useful thing this page could do.

Each document carries its own version and effective date, and that line is the authoritative one. This page is the index to them.

8 October 2026

  • Privacy Policy → version 1.4 and Terms of Service → version 1.4, both effective from this date. The version line at the top of each document is the authoritative one.
  • Child & Young Athlete Safety → 1.3 and Your Data Rights → 1.2.
  • Deleting Your Account → 1.1. Deletion is now described step for step as the app actually performs it, including the confirmation phrase and the verification the app runs afterwards.
  • AI & Coaching Disclaimer, AI Training Advice and AI Data Use → 1.1, and the health, injury, mental wellbeing, nutrition and contact notices → 1.1.
  • Added to this website: a questions and answers page, an accessibility statement, and this page.
  • Removed from this website: the screen-by-screen app preview pages and the device screenshots they carried.
  • Speed: the stylesheet every page blocks on now ships minified — about 60% smaller after compression, with nothing removed that a reader sees.

7 October 2026

  • Advertising & Tracking Disclosure → 1.2, restating exactly which advertising identifiers and SDKs are absent from the build.
  • Cookie Policy → 1.1.
  • The app's cookie and storage notice → version dated 7 October 2026. It reports a fact rather than asking for consent, because there is nothing here to consent to: the site sets no tracking cookie at all.

6 October 2026

  • A security pass over this website added a Content-Security-Policy, HSTS and a Permissions-Policy to every response. The policy was verified against what the site actually loads, which is why it can be strict: there are no inline scripts, no inline styles beyond what React and the legal viewer need, and no evaluation anywhere in the build.

4 October 2026

  • Credits, Subscriptions & Virtual Currency → 1.3.
  • Refunds & Cancellation → 1.2 and Waiting List Terms → 1.2.

2 October 2026

  • Seven documents published for the first time at version 1.0: Competitions & Promotion Terms, the complaints procedure, reporting content or a user, reporting a safety concern, security and vulnerability reporting, the video analysis disclaimer, and the wearable and health data notice.

1 October 2026

  • Community Guidelines and Intellectual Property published at version 1.0, the first two documents on the site.

Why bother

A policy you cannot tell has changed is not much of a policy

Most sites update a privacy policy silently and leave the date at the top. This page exists so that the question “what changed, and was it something I should care about” has an answer that does not involve diffing two versions yourself.
