For athletes in every sport

A training log that tells you what your own numbers mean.

Vaylo Sports records what you actually train, then works it back to you: load, readiness, recovery, and the limiter most likely holding you back. It works across running, cycling, swimming, team sports, racket sports, combat sports and everything in between.

It is a tool for organising and understanding your own training. It is not a doctor, not a physio, and not a coach — and it will tell you when to stop rather than push on.

Straight answers

What works, what does not, and what is switched off

Most apps of this kind blur that line. Vaylo does not, so here it is before you install anything.

Working today

26 features across 6 areas — logging, metrics, the Limiter Test, injury scaffolding, nutrition, the Learning Hub, event packs and the community side. Open the app and they run.

Built, not yet switched on

6 AI features. The server capability that runs them is not enabled, so the app says so when you open them. We list them rather than pretend.

Deliberately off

3 integrations. Wearable sync, GPS and the coach marketplace are disabled for stated reasons. No data moves to or from them.

The app

What you actually get

A summary. The full list, with the honest status of each feature, is on the features page.

Train and log

  • Performance Log. Log sessions with RPE, duration, notes and form. It is the record everything else is calculated from.
  • Training plans. Week-by-week plans built from what you have logged, your sport and your availability.
  • Routines. Your own repeatable sessions, with history, so a good week is not rebuilt from memory.
  • Goals and event preparation. Set goals, track them against your log, and work towards a specific event.

Understand your training

  • Performance Dashboard. Readiness, acute load, A:C ratio, calories, protein and an injury-risk flag, calculated from your own entries.
  • VPR performance rating. A single rating built from the metrics you log. Arithmetic on your data — not a prediction and not AI.
  • Limiter Test. Find which limiter is holding you back, then work on that one rather than guessing.
  • Development trajectory. A trend line across your own VPR history. It describes what happened; it does not forecast what will.
  • Equipment Tracker. Log the gear you train on and rotate it before it becomes the problem.

Recovery, injury and the body

  • Recovery tracking. Sleep, soreness, mood and energy, and how they read next to your training load.
  • Injury Management. A generic return-to-play scaffold to organise a rehab you have already been given. It does not diagnose, and it carries a permanent notice telling you to see a physiotherapist or doctor.
  • Nutrition. Calorie and protein tracking against your training. Restricted to athletes aged 18 and over.
  • Form analysis. Upload video of a lift or movement and review it frame by frame against your own earlier sets.

Skill and tactics

  • Learning Hub. Written lessons, drills and quizzes across sports, including load management for growing athletes.
  • Tactical analysis. Organise opponents, read matchups and keep notes on what worked.
  • Cross-training. Work out what a second sport is doing for your first one, and where the two are pulling against each other.
  • Compare. Put two periods side by side and see what actually changed.

Why it is built this way

Three things Vaylo will not do

It will not diagnose you

Injury Management organises a rehab a professional has already given you. It does not diagnose, and it carries a permanent on-screen notice telling you to see a physiotherapist or doctor for sharp, persistent or worsening pain.

It will not feed your data to advertisers

There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no advertising identifier in the app. Sponsored placements are 18+, always labelled, and report back to brands as anonymous counts — never your name, location or training data.

Advertising & tracking disclosure

It will not hide a limitation

Where a number is plain arithmetic on what you logged, we say so. Where a sentence was written by a language model, we say that too — and explain why it can be confidently wrong.

AI & coaching disclaimer

Safety

Built for younger athletes as well as adults

For athletes under 18, Vaylo switches off the things a young person can be harmed by, and caps training volume by age. None of it is a substitute for a parent, a coach or a doctor.

No advertising

Never shown to anyone under 18. Enforced by the database, not the interface.

No nutrition advice

Calorie and weight features are blocked for under-18s, with an explanation.

Private by default

A minor's profile cannot be made public at all.

Age-capped volume

Sessions, minutes and rest days capped by age band.
