01 The story

Built in the open,
slowly on purpose.

Vaylo Sports is not a studio's product. It is one build, started from an idea in 2024, written through 2025 and 2026, and launching in 2027.

That pace is deliberate. A training app is trusted with the one thing an athlete has least of — recovery capacity — so the features that could do harm were the last ones added and the first ones constrained.

The full account, including what is still missing, is on the About page.

2024

The idea. Vaylo started as a question rather than a product: if a training log already knows every session you have done, why is it still only showing you a calendar? The idea was to make the record answer back.

2025

The first build. Building began, one screen at a time: log a session, read the load it added, keep the plan honest against the week that actually happened. Everything that could be arithmetic on your own data was built as arithmetic, not as a guess dressed up as AI.

2026Now

Still building. The year spent turning a working app into a product that can be trusted: the safety rules for younger athletes, the documents behind every claim, the parts of the app that should say no. It is slower than adding features, and it is the reason the app can be launched at all.

2027

Launch. The Android release opens in 2027. The waiting list is open now, and it is used for one thing: telling you when that happens.

What it actually does

02 What it does

Six things, in the order an athlete meets them. The full inventory, including what is built but not switched on, is on the features page.

01

Log the session

Sport, duration, RPE, notes, how it felt. The Performance Log is the record every other number is calculated from.

 02

Read the load

Acute load, the acute-to-chronic ratio, readiness and recovery, read against your own history rather than a population average.

 03

Find the limiter

A test that names the one thing most likely to be holding you back, so the next block of training has a target.

 04

Prepare for the event

Structured programmes towards a date, with a taper and race-day guidance, plus recurring routines for the weeks in between.

 05

Recover on purpose

Sleep, soreness, mood and energy beside the load that produced them — and an injury scaffold that organises rehab you were already given, without diagnosing anything.

 06

Learn the sport

Written lessons, drills and quizzes, including load management for growing athletes, plus tactical notes on the opponents you actually face.

Straight answers

What works, what does not, and what is switched off

Most apps of this kind blur that line. Vaylo does not, so here it is before you install anything.

Working today

26 features across 6 areas — logging, metrics, the Limiter Test, injury scaffolding, nutrition, the Learning Hub, event packs and the community side. Open the app and they run.

Built, not yet switched on

6 AI features. The server capability that runs them is not enabled, so the app says so when you open them. We list them rather than pretend.

Deliberately off

3 integrations. Wearable sync, GPS and the coach marketplace are disabled for stated reasons. No data moves to or from them.

Why it is built this way

Three things Vaylo will not do

It will not diagnose you

Injury Management organises a rehab a professional has already given you. It does not diagnose, and it carries a permanent on-screen notice telling you to see a physiotherapist or doctor for sharp, persistent or worsening pain.

It will not feed your data to advertisers

There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no advertising identifier in the app. Sponsored placements are 18+, always labelled, and report back to brands as anonymous counts — never your name, location or training data.

Advertising & tracking disclosure

It will not hide a limitation

Where a number is plain arithmetic on what you logged, we say so. Where a sentence was written by a language model, we say that too — and explain why it can be confidently wrong.

AI & coaching disclaimer

Safety

Built for younger athletes as well as adults

For athletes under 18, Vaylo switches off the things a young person can be harmed by, and caps training volume by age. None of it is a substitute for a parent, a coach or a doctor.

No advertising

Never shown to anyone under 18. Enforced by the database, not the interface.

No nutrition advice

Calorie and weight features are blocked for under-18s, with an explanation.

Private by default

A minor's profile cannot be made public at all.

Age-capped volume

Sessions, minutes and rest days capped by age band.
