For brands

Put your name on what athletes actually do.

Vaylo Sports is a training log — the place an athlete records the work and reads back what it means. A sponsorship here sits beside that record: a branded challenge, an achievement, a card where it belongs.

It is not a data business, and it is not an ad network. Read the four limits below first — they are commitments we have already published, so they are worth knowing before you spend a minute on the questionnaire.

Start the questionnaire Advertising disclosure

Six answers · replies from a person · no charge

  • No athlete-level data. Aggregate impressions and clicks only.
  • 18+ only. Enforced in the database, not the interface.
  • Always labelled. A sponsored card says it is sponsored.
  • No influence on advice. Not for sale at any price.

What you can buy

Four things exist, two do not yet

We would rather show you the real state of the product than a capability list. Sponsored challenges and branded achievements run today. Three placement slots are built but not yet placed on a page. The AI slot is a data model and nothing more.

The limits

What sponsorship cannot buy here

These are not negotiating positions. They are published commitments, and a deal that needed one of them removed is a deal we would have to turn down.

You never see an athlete

No name, no location, no training data, no health data — not in a report, not in a dashboard, not "anonymised" in a way that is reversible. What you get is aggregate counts: how many times a card was shown, and how many times it was clicked. That is the whole reporting surface, and it has no user column to leak.

You never reach an under-18

Sponsored content is hidden from anybody without an adult date of birth, and that is enforced by the database rather than the interface — so it cannot be switched off by a client, an update, or a mistake on a screen. Note the honest limitation: age is self-declared, which is true of every age gate.

Your money never touches the coaching

A placement is a card, labelled Sponsored. It is not advice and it cannot become advice. Nothing you buy changes what the app calculates, what a plan says, or what an AI feature writes to an athlete about their own training — and no amount of budget changes that, because the promise is already published.

Every placement is labelled

Each card carries a disclosure label, and the label is required rather than decorative. If a deal needs the label removed to be worth doing, it is not a deal we can do.

After you send it

What actually happens next

  1. Your answers are stored, and an email goes to a person — not to a queue nobody reads.
  2. You get a reply to the address you gave, from a human, usually with questions rather than a price list.
  3. If it goes further, a deal becomes real by hand: a brand record, a placement, a start and end date and a disclosure label. Nothing appears in the app before that.
  4. Reporting afterwards is aggregate counts. You will never receive a list of who saw your card.

On "AI preference toward your brand". It is on the questionnaire because brands ask for it, and it is marked not implemented because that is the truth — the database can hold the row, and nothing reads it. Even where it exists later, a paid preference inside an athlete's coaching is the one thing this product will not sell: an athlete must be able to trust that the advice about their own body was written for them. If that is the only thing you are looking for, this is the wrong product, and we would rather say so now.
