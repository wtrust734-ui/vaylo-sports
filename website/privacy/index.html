Privacy & data

What we hold, and how to get rid of it

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy. This page is the short version, with the actions that matter.

Downloading and deleting are self-service. Both are one tap in the app, both are instant, and neither needs us to approve it. You do not have to email anybody.

Your actions

What you can do, and how

Get a copy

A JSON file of everything we hold, downloaded straight to your device.

Profile → Legal & Safety Centre → Download your data

If any part cannot be read, the app tells you exactly which and why, rather than sending a partial file quietly.

Correct it

Edit your profile and any training entry directly in the app. No request needed.

If something is wrong that you cannot edit, ask and we will fix it.

Delete it

Delete your account and every table that can hold your data, verified afterwards.

How deletion works

Anything else — restricting use, objecting, withdrawing consent — is an email or the form below. We respond within 30 days, free, wherever you live.

Advertising and analytics

What tracking exists

None. Vaylo Sports contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracker, and no advertising identifier. The app does not read Google's Advertising ID.

The only advertising in the app is four sponsored placements. Each is always labelled, each is shown to adults only — enforced by a database constraint, not a screen — and each reports back to the brand as anonymous per-day counts of impressions and taps.

  • A brand never receives your name, contact details, location, training data, health data, sport, age or any identifier.
  • Athletes under 18 are excluded from sponsor reporting entirely.
  • Your health data is never used to select, personalise or measure advertising.

Children

If you are under 18

  • Advertising is never shown to you, and your activity is never counted in sponsor reporting.
  • Nutrition and calorie features are switched off entirely.
  • Your profile is private by default and cannot be made public.
  • Training volume is capped by your age band, with more frequent deload weeks.

A parent or guardian can ask us to delete your account at any time, without giving a reason. Child & young athlete safety

You can also complain

You do not have to come to us first. In the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at any time, and elsewhere in the EU to the authority where you live.

Nothing in our terms prevents you complaining, to us or to anyone else.

One thing we have not settled

The exact retention period for server logs — which can include your IP address and device details — is not yet decided, so it is not published as a number. Your account data is kept until you delete it, and purchase records for as long as tax law requires.
