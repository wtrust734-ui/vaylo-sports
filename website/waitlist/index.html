Waiting list

Join the waiting list

Vaylo Sports is not on the Google Play Store yet. It opens on 1 January 2027, and there is no listing to link to until then. Join the waiting list and we will email you when that happens: a confirmation now, a short run of updates as the date approaches, and the link on launch day. That is the whole of what the address is used for.

Joining costs nothing and reserves nothing, and the waiting list terms say exactly what it does and does not mean — in one page, in plain words.

Join

One field, and what we do with it

We store the address you give here and the time you gave it, and we use it for one thing: telling you about the Android release. The confirmation goes out as soon as you join, and it names the date we are working to — 1 January 2027 — rather than implying one is close.

Join the waiting list

One field. We will use the address for one thing: telling you when the Android release opens.

Nothing else is required, and we will not add you to a marketing list.

Your address is used for one thing: telling you when the Android release opens. It is not a mailing list, it is not shared with anyone, and you can have it removed at any time — see the waiting list terms and the Privacy & Data Centre.

What happens to your address

  • It is stored securely in our own database.
  • It is used for one thing: a confirmation when you join, a short run of updates before launch, and the message with the link when the Android release opens.
  • It is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. There is no advertising on this site.
  • It is not added to any other list, because there are no other lists.
  • Every email carries a link that removes you from the list immediately.
  • The waiting list terms set this out in full, including how to have it deleted.

Joining twice does not create two entries, and we will not email you to ask whether you meant to.

Changed your mind?

Use the link at the foot of any email we send and you are removed immediately — there is nothing to confirm and no reason to give. You can also ask us through the contact page or the support form, which carries the same 30-day commitment as every other data request — in practice it is immediate.

That link is unique to you, and there is no page where an address can be typed in to remove somebody. That is deliberate: a form that acted on any address entered would let anyone check whether a given person is on this list, and let anyone remove them. A per-person link does neither.

Meanwhile

You do not have to wait

Most of Vaylo does not need the Play Store. The app runs in your browser against the same backend, so you can look around, or check whether it is worth waiting for.
