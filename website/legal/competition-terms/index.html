Competitions & Promotion Terms

Version 1.0 · 2 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports · Contact: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

The short version. Competitions and referral promotions are a fun part of the app. They are not a gambling product, they are not a way to win money, and they do not cost you anything to enter. Unless a specific competition says otherwise in its own announcement, a reward is virtual currency you can spend inside Vaylo Sports and nothing more.

What competitions and promotions are

Vaylo Sports runs challenges, leaderboards and referral promotions. A challenge is a target you can opt into — a distance, a number of sessions, a target time. A leaderboard ranks athletes who have opted into the same challenge. A referral promotion credits you when someone you invited signs up and meets the published condition.

Each challenge has its own announcement in the app, and that announcement is part of these terms. Where this page and a challenge announcement disagree, the announcement governs for that challenge.

Entering, and what it costs

Entering a challenge or a promotion is free and voluntary. You opt in; we do not enter you automatically. You can leave a leaderboard at any time and your rank is removed with you.

No entry fee, no purchase requirement. Nothing in this app requires you to spend credits, subscribe, or buy anything in order to enter a competition or to keep your existing rank and rewards. If a future promotion ever did require a purchase to enter, that would be a different kind of product and we would describe it explicitly and separately rather than bury it here.

What a reward actually is

Unless a specific announcement says otherwise in cash terms, a reward is virtual currency: coins or credits that exist only inside Vaylo Sports. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, exchanged, sold or transferred, and are not money. We can change what they buy, and we can change or withdraw them, without notice, because they are not a contractual benefit.

This is not a prize draw, a lottery or a game of chance. Outcomes are determined by your own logged activity, not by chance. Nothing here is a mechanism for gambling, and we will not run a competition whose result depends on chance for money.

Where a promotion does offer something with real-world value, the announcement will say so plainly, including any eligibility, time limit and condition. We are not going to describe a reward in a way that leaves you guessing whether it is money.

How we keep it fair

A competition result depends on the activity you logged. To keep that honest we rely on the activity records in your own account, and we may check them. We look for, among other things:

  • activity that appears to have been entered by something other than you;
  • the same session or activity counted more than once;
  • a referral recorded from an account you control, or a self-referral;
  • any other manipulation of the numbers a result depends on.

If we believe a result was obtained by manipulation we may remove the rank, withhold the reward, and close the account. We will tell you what we did and why. If you think that is wrong, the complaints procedure applies.

We can end a competition early. If a challenge is not working, if it has been compromised, or if it is no longer something we can run fairly, we can stop it. We will try to notice the participants first. Rewards already credited stay credited unless they were obtained by manipulation.

Competitions and under-18s

Athletes aged 13 to 17 can enter challenges. Nothing about a competition is switched off for them, because a challenge is just your own logged training.

What is switched off for under-18s is advertising and anything involving spending money, which is why a reward for an under-18 athlete is always virtual currency they can spend in the app and never a cash payout. See Child & Young Athlete Safety.

No guarantee, and no reliance

Competitions are provided as-is. A leaderboard position is a record of what the app calculated, not a certified measure of athletic ability, and we do not warrant that a leaderboard is accurate, complete, error-free or always available. We may correct, reorder or remove entries.

Nothing in a competition or promotion is medical, coaching or professional advice, and nothing in it should be treated as a reason to change your training. See Training & Health Safety.

Changes to these terms

We may change these terms, and we may change or withdraw any competition or promotion. If a change affects a competition you have already entered and not yet completed, we will try to tell you in the app before it takes effect.
