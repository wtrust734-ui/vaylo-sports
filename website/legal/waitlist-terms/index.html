Waiting List Terms

Version 1.2 · 4 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports · Contact: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

The short version. The waiting list is a note that you want the app. It costs nothing, it is not a pre-order, and it does not reserve anything. We keep the email address you give us and one thing is done with it: telling you when the Android release opens. There is no queue and no position, so joining does not put you ahead of anybody. The Android release is set for 1 January 2027, and if that date moves you will be told before the day.

What the waiting list is, and what it is not

The waiting list is a record that you are interested in Vaylo Sports. You give us an email address; we keep it so that we can tell you when the Android release is available to install.

It is not a pre-order, a reservation, a booking, a subscription or a purchase. Joining it does not create an account, does not give you access to the app, and does not entitle you to anything. It also does not commit us to releasing anything, on any platform, at any time.

What it costs, and what you get

Joining is free. There is no fee, no deposit, no card, and nothing to cancel, because there is nothing to pay. We do not take payment details on the waiting list page at all.

Nothing on the waiting list is a product, a service or a benefit that you are buying, so nothing here is a contractual entitlement. Nobody who joins is charged, and nobody who joins owes us anything.

What we store, and what we do with it

When you join, we store:

  • the email address you type in;
  • your name, only if you choose to give it — it is optional and the form works without it;
  • the date and time you joined;
  • a short, salted, one-way hash of the network address the request came from, used only to stop one source signing up hundreds of times. It is not stored in a readable form and is not used to identify you.

It is used for one thing and nothing else: telling you about the Android release. That is a confirmation when you join, a short run of updates in the weeks before the release opens, and the launch-day message with the link. It is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers, and it is not added to any other list, because there are no other lists. The full detail of how we handle personal data, your rights, and how to exercise them is in the Privacy Policy.

What you will actually receive. A confirmation when you join, then a short countdown in the run-up to launch — three updates, one about a month before, one a week before, and one the day before — and finally the launch-day message with the link. That is the whole of it: there is no newsletter and nothing else queued. The launch date is 1 January 2027, and if it moves the updates will say so rather than repeat a date that is no longer true.

You can leave at any time, without giving a reason — every email we send carries a link that removes you immediately. See Leaving the list below. There is still no page where an address can be typed in to remove somebody, and the reason is worth stating: a form that acted on any address entered would let anyone check whether a given person is on this list, and would let anyone remove them. A link that is unique to one person does neither.

No place, no priority, and the one date we do give

There is no queue. The waiting list is a list of people who want the app, not a line that is served in order, so joining does not give you a position and does not put you ahead of anybody else. We do not publish a number of places and there is no number to publish.

The Android release is set for 1 January 2027. That is a real date we are working to, not a placeholder to keep you interested, but it is not a guarantee: it can still move, and if it does you will be told before the day rather than after it. Joining the waiting list does not entitle you to the app on that date and does not make the date a contractual promise. Features described elsewhere in this website describe what the app does today, not what any future release will contain.

We may close the waiting list, stop accepting new entries, or stop operating the list altogether. If we do, we will still honour a deletion request.

Leaving the list

Every email we send carries an unsubscribe link. It is unique to you, it removes this address from the list immediately, and you do not have to give a reason or confirm anything beyond pressing the button. Nothing is sent to you afterwards, including the message about the Android release.

You can also tell us through the Contact page or the complaints route and we will do the same thing. We treat that as a data request, so it carries the same 30-day commitment as every other one — in practice it is immediate, and you do not have to give a reason.

Removal is recorded rather than the entry vanishing: the address is marked as removed and is never mailed again, which is what makes it possible to show that a request was honoured.

Deleting your address from the waiting list does not affect a Vaylo account if you have one, and deleting an account does not remove you from the waiting list. They are separate, and the account deletion page covers the other one.

Age

Vaylo Sports is intended for adult athletes aged 18 to 30, and the waiting list is for the athletes the app is for. It is not age-gated, and our Terms set the minimum age for holding an account at 13, so the list is open from that age. If you are under the age at which you can consent to online services where you live, please ask a parent or guardian before joining, or let them join on your behalf. We do not knowingly collect an address from a child below that age; if you believe one has been given to us, tell us and we will delete it. The child safety document sets out how the app itself treats athletes under 18.

Changes to these terms

We may change these terms, and we may change or withdraw the waiting list. When we change them we will update the version and date at the top of this page. Because the only thing we hold is an email address, and because we are not promising you anything, a change here cannot take away a benefit you have already been given.

These terms sit alongside the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. If anything here conflicts with the Terms of Service, the Terms of Service govern. Nothing in this page limits any right you have under the law of your country that cannot lawfully be limited, and nothing here excludes our liability for anything that cannot lawfully be excluded.
