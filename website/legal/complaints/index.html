Complaints Procedure

Version 1.0 · 2 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports · Contact: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

The short version. If something has gone wrong, tell us and a person will read it. Most complaints are resolved by a reply. If you are not happy with how we handled it, you can ask us to look again, and you can complain to a regulator. We would much rather hear the complaint from you.
On this page
  1. Step one — tell us
  2. Step two — our response
  3. Step three — ask us to look again
  4. Step four — a regulator
  5. What we are not able to do

Step one — tell us

Email vaylosportssupport@gmail.com, or use the in-app support route if the problem is about your account. Put Complaint in the subject line.

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and what you would like instead. If something went wrong with your data, your account, a purchase or the app, say which — it goes to a different person.

A complaint is handled differently from a support question. It is logged as a complaint, it is reviewed rather than answered off the cuff, and you will get a response that addresses the complaint itself.

If the issue is a safety concern, do not use this route — use Report a Safety Concern, which is treated more urgently. If it is a security flaw, use Security & Vulnerability Reporting.

Step two — our response

You will get an acknowledgement, and then a substantive response once someone has looked at it properly.

We aim to acknowledge a complaint within five working days and to respond substantively within twenty. Those are aims we are setting out to meet, not a contractual deadline, and we are not going to dress them up as a guarantee. If a complaint is going to take longer — because it needs a database query, or a payment provider, or a legal question — we will say so and tell you why rather than go quiet.

Where we got something wrong, we will say so. Where we do not agree with you, we will explain why rather than repeat the same paragraph.

Step three — ask us to look again

If you disagree with the outcome, reply to the same email and ask for a review. That request goes to someone who was not involved in the original decision.

Please tell us what you think we got wrong and what you would expect instead, because a review that starts from the same information will reach the same answer. If there is information we did not have — a screenshot, a transaction reference, something that happened off-platform — this is the moment to send it.

We will confirm the review outcome and explain the reasoning. A review is the last step inside Vaylo Sports.

Step four — a regulator

You are not required to come to us first, and nothing on this page is a condition of your right to complain elsewhere.

If you are in the UK, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk about how we have handled your personal data. We have not yet published our ICO registration number or registered address, which is itself a gap we are aware of; the ICO's register is public and searchable, and the Privacy Policy says the same thing.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may complain to the supervisory authority for your country. Our representative for the UK and EEA has not been appointed, which the Privacy Policy also discloses.

If this is a consumer contract issue — a purchase, a refund, a subscription — you may also have rights to take the matter further. The Terms of Service sets out the governing law, which is the law of the country in which Vaylo Sports is established, and that is stated there rather than here because we have not published our registered details.

Nothing in these terms limits any right or remedy you have under the law of your country, and nothing here requires you to contact us before using them.

What we are not able to do

Stated plainly, because a complaints procedure that overstates itself is worse than none:

  • We do not operate an internal ombudsman and have no external one. A review by someone uninvolved is the most we offer.
  • We do not have a published response-time guarantee. The aims above are aims.
  • We cannot compensate beyond what the Terms of Service provide for a purchase, and we cannot invent goodwill payments as a matter of policy.
  • We cannot investigate matters outside the app, or act on another person, on a complaint alone.
  • We do not yet have a published complaints code we are accredited to.

We do not retaliate against anyone for complaining, and we do not treat a complaint about us as a reason to close an account.
