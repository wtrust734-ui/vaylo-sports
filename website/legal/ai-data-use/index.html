AI Data Use & Processing Notice

Version 1.1 · 8 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports · Contact: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

The short version. When an AI feature runs, it sends the minimum it needs — your training numbers and the question being asked — through our own server to an AI provider. Your account credentials, your private messages and your payment details are never sent. You can see exactly what goes, and turn the features off.

Vaylo Sports is intended for adult athletes aged 18–30, and the AI features described here are built for them. That is not a technical age gate — age is not verified — and where an account shows an athlete is under 18, the age-based features and limits described in Child & Young Athlete Safety stay switched on.

Current status

The AI features are not currently enabled in the production app. They need an external AI provider and we have not deployed one, so they fail closed with an error rather than returning something invented. This notice describes what the features send when they are enabled, so the description is accurate before anyone relies on it.

How a request travels

A generated response is never fetched by your phone directly from an AI provider. The call goes:

  1. your app calls our server, authenticated as you;
  2. our server checks you are allowed to use the feature;
  3. our server sends the AI provider only the fields that feature needs;
  4. the provider's response returns to our server, which passes the text back to you.

The provider never sees your account password, your session, your email address, your payment details, or your community posts. It sees a request containing training numbers and a prompt.

What is sent, feature by feature

FeatureSent to the providerNever sent
Suggested training plan Your recent sessions, volume and load figures, and any age band Name, email, password, payments, community content
Weekly review Your sessions and load for the week Your friends list, your reports, your messages
Coaching chat The text you typed and recent training context Anything from another athlete; the provider cannot see your feed
Video form feedback The clip you chose and the movement being analysed Your identity, your other clips, anything unconnected to the analysis
Natural language goals The goal text you typed Everything else

For under-18s the features that could produce nutrition or calorie content are not available at all, so nothing is sent for them. See Child & Young Athlete Safety.

Whether your data is used to train models

This is the question people actually care about, so here is a precise answer rather than a reassuring one.

What we have designed for is that your data is not used to train a general-purpose model. Requests are made through our own server to a provider account, and we have intended to configure that account so submitted content is not used for training.

We are not able to state this as a verified fact yet, and we would rather say so. We have not yet completed the provider's contractual and configuration review, so we cannot currently evidence the claim. Before these features are enabled for anyone, that review has to be done and this section rewritten to state what is actually true.

We will not quietly change this section. If it becomes possible for content to be used for training, that belongs in here in plain words before it happens, not in a policy update afterwards.

Your control

  • Do not use an AI feature and nothing is sent. Every one of them is optional.
  • Do not type anything into an AI feature you would not want processed. It is a prompt to a third party, and we are telling you so rather than hoping you assume it.
  • Export or delete everything, generated content included, through Your Data Rights or Deleting Your Account.

Generated responses are shown to you in the app and are not published to your profile or the feed. Do not treat a generated response as a fact about you, including the ones that flatter you — they are generated from patterns, and a plausible-sounding wrong answer is the normal failure mode.

See also AI & Coaching Disclaimer and AI Training Advice Disclaimer.
