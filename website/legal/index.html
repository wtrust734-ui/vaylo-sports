Delete your data

How to delete everything, from inside the app

You do not need to email us, and you do not need to give a reason. Deletion is self-service in the Android app, it takes effect immediately, and the app reports what it removed rather than claiming success.

  1. Open Vaylo Sports and sign in. Deletion is tied to your account, so it has to be your session.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account permanently. It sits under Sign Out. The same screen is reachable from Profile → Legal & Safety → Delete your account.
  4. Confirm it is you. The next screen asks for it deliberately, so a session left open on a shared phone cannot destroy somebody else's account. Enter your password and tap Verify — or, if you do not sign in with a password, tap Re-authenticate and sign in with the provider you normally use.
  5. Type DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Exactly that, in capitals.
  6. Tick the acknowledgement box that says you understand the deletion is permanent and cannot be recovered.
  7. Tap Delete Account, then Permanently Delete in the confirmation that appears.
  8. Nothing else is needed. The account, its login and every table that can hold your data are deleted straight away, and you are signed out.

Do these two things first

Download your data. Profile → Legal & Safety → Download your data produces a JSON file of everything we hold: profile, training, health, social and purchase records. It takes seconds, and it cannot be done afterwards.

Cancel any Google Play subscription. Deleting your Vaylo account does not cancel a subscription bought through Google Play, and an app you have deleted is not somewhere you can cancel it from. Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.

What is removed, and the three things that are not

Everything is removed: your profile, every training session and derived metric, connected health data, posts, communities, challenges, messages, friendships, uploads, purchase and entitlement records, reports you filed, and the sign-in itself.

Three things survive, and the Account Deletion notice names each one:

  • One anonymous deletion record — a salted hash of your user id, a status and a timestamp. No name, no email, and nothing the account can be rebuilt from.
  • Purchase and tax records, kept for as long as accounting law requires, separate from your profile and no longer linked to your user id.
  • Nothing else. If you believe something of yours survived that is not on that list, tell us and we will check it rather than reassure you.

If you cannot get into the app

Email the address on the account from that address if you still have it, and we will delete the account and confirm when it is done. That route is a request rather than an instant action, because we have to verify you are the account owner before destroying anything.
