2. What you can ask for

Under UK and EU data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access — confirmation of whether we hold data about you, and a copy of it.
  • Rectification — correct anything inaccurate.
  • Erasure — delete it ("the right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — stop us using it while a dispute is resolved.
  • Portability — get it in a machine-readable format you can take elsewhere.
  • Objection — object to particular uses, including profiling and direct marketing. We do not do direct marketing, so there is nothing to object to on that basis.
  • Withdraw consent — change your mind about any optional permission. Withdrawal does not undo processing that already happened.
  • Not be subject to automated decisions with legal or similar significant effects. We make no such decisions about you. There is no automated scoring in Vaylo Sports that decides whether you get access to anything, whether you are a suspect, or anything comparable.
  • Complain to your data protection regulator — see section 7.

These rights apply regardless of where you live, and we extend them to everybody we serve rather than only to those in the UK and EU. We do not charge for exercising them.

3. A copy of your data

The in-app export is a full copy, not a summary. It covers your account and profile, training and session history, measurements, goals, media and uploads, any content you have posted, communities, challenges, messages, your purchase history, your consent records, and the reports you have made.

It is produced as a JSON file, downloaded straight to your device.

4. The full export, and what happens if it is incomplete

We will tell you if an export is incomplete rather than quietly sending you a partial file. If any part of your data cannot be read, the export still completes but is marked incomplete, and it lists exactly which parts are missing and why. If you see that notice, email us and we will finish it by hand.
  • You can only export your own data. The request is authorised by your own login, and there is no way to ask for someone else's.
  • Your export is not shared with anyone — not with brands, not with our AI providers, not with anyone.
  • Exported data includes health and wellness information if you have connected it. Treat the file as sensitive and do not post it publicly.

5. Deleting your account

Deletion is available in the app at any time, without asking us and without a reason. It removes your profile, training history, health data, content, messages, purchases and uploaded files, and removes the login itself.

  • The app verifies the wipe afterwards by checking every table that can hold your data and confirming nothing remains.
  • One record survives: a salted hash of your user id, a status and a timestamp, with no name, email or identifier. It exists only to prove the deletion happened, and it does not let anyone reconstruct your account.
  • Deleting is not undoable. Download your data first if you want a copy.

Full detail is in Deleting Your Account.

6. Asking us by email

Email vaylosportssupport@gmail.com for anything not in the table above.

Include enough for us to find you — the account email address or display name, plus what you want. If the request is about someone else's account, say so.

We commit to responding within 30 days. That is the statutory period, we are committing to the full period rather than a portion of it, and we do not charge. If a request is complex enough that we need longer, we will tell you why within those 30 days.

If you are not the person named in the account — for example, reporting a child account or asking about someone else's data — say so and explain your relationship. We will not disclose anyone's data to a casual caller, and we will not ignore a credible safeguarding concern either.

7. Complaining

  • Complain to us first at vaylosportssupport@gmail.com. We would rather hear it directly.
  • You do not have to. If you are in the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) at any time, and if you are elsewhere in the EU, to the data protection authority in the country where you live.
  • Nothing in our terms prevents you complaining, to us or to anyone else. A term that tried to stop that would be unenforceable under UK consumer law.
One gap we should be honest about. Vaylo Sports's own ICO registration number and its registered postal address have not yet been inserted into these documents, because we will not publish a number we cannot verify. Until they are, you can still complain to the ICO directly — your right to complain does not depend on us having published a number, and we will not ask you to wait for our details.

8. Parents, guardians and under-18s

The app is intended for 18–30-year-old athletes. It is not age-gated, and an athlete of 13 or over can hold an account, so this section applies to any account whose date of birth shows it belongs to someone under 18.

  • A parent or guardian can ask us to delete an under-18 account at any time, with no reason required. Email us.
  • Athletes under 18 can exercise their own rights in the app, the same as any other user. We do not require adult permission to access your own data.
  • Guardian links are opt-in and belong to the athlete. The athlete creates a single-use code in the app, the guardian redeems it, and the link stays pending until the athlete approves it. The link record holds the two account ids, the relationship label, the permissions granted, the link status, and the invite code's issue and expiry times. A linked guardian sees a reduced summary — load and recovery bands and safety alerts, never the training log — and the athlete can change or revoke it at any time. See Child & Young Athlete Safety.
  • A parent or guardian without a link can still make a request. Say who you are and what your relationship to the account holder is, as section 6 describes. We will not disclose data to a casual caller, and we will not ignore a safeguarding concern.
  • Purchases on an under-18 account. Any purchase made on an under-18 account is refunded in full when it is called up — whoever asks, and whatever the circumstances. See Refunds & Cancellation.

9. What we have not yet finalised

  • Exact retention periods. As of 20 October 2026 these are published in the Privacy Policy, section 8: server logs 30 days rolling; unsaved form analyses and AI usage records 30 days, enforced by a scheduled server-side purge; purchase records up to six years after the last transaction; support correspondence 24 months after your request is closed. Account data lasts until you delete it.
  • Controller identity. Registered company name, company number, registered address and ICO number, as above.
  • Whether a data protection representative is required — that depends on who the controller turns out to be.
  • Whether existing adult profiles get privatised retroactively. New accounts are private by default and under-18 accounts are always private, but accounts created before that change may still be public. Vaylo Sports has not decided whether to change them, and a decision either way is owed to existing users.

Listing these is not an admission that anything is unlawful. It is us telling you where the edges of the policy currently are, because a document that claimed to be complete would not be true.
