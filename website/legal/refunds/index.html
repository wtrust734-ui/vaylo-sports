Refunds & Cancellation

Version 1.2 · 4 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports · Contact: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

Buying is not currently switched on. No purchase can be made in the app right now, so in practice this page is about what will happen when checkout is enabled, and about the commitments that apply regardless. If you believe you have been charged by something calling itself Vaylo Sports, email us straight away — we will check it.

1. Who you go to first

On Android, Google Play takes the payment, so Google's refund policy and its process are the first route for anything to do with the charge itself. Request a refund through Google from the purchase in the Play Store.

Contact us as well at vaylosportssupport@gmail.com, because we are the ones who can see what you actually bought and what you actually got. Include:

  • your account email address or display name;
  • the date and the amount charged, and the order number from Google Play;
  • what you expected and what happened instead;
  • any screenshot of the error, which usually saves a round trip.

2. Cancelling a subscription

  • Cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. We cannot cancel it for you, because Google holds the subscription.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal and lets the period you have already paid for run to its end. You keep full access until then.
  • Cancelling is not a refund. If you want your money back, that is a separate request — see below.
  • Uninstalling the app does not cancel anything. So does deleting your Vaylo account.
  • One-off purchases and "lifetime" purchases cannot be cancelled, because they are not subscriptions. If one is faulty, ask us.

3. Getting a refund

There are two separate things you might be asking for, and conflating them wastes everyone's time:

What happenedWhere to go
You were charged and want the money backGoogle Play for the charge; us for anything about the service
The feature never worked after you paid for itUs — email us and we will look at it
You bought credits and have not spent themUs, then us
You spent credits on a feature that then failedUs — we will return the credits spent
You want a refund because you no longer want the appGoogle Play, and note that once credits have been used you may not get money back — but see your rights below

4. When we will refund you

We commit to the following:

  • A purchase was made by, or for, someone under 18. If you call it up, we refund it in full. That applies whoever asks — the under-18 athlete or a parent or guardian — and no reason is required. It does not depend on anything else on this page, on whether the feature was used, or on how much time has passed beyond what Google Play's own process allows.
  • We failed to deliver the feature you paid for, or the feature was materially broken and we did not fix it. You get the purchase refunded or the credits returned.
  • We were charged twice for the same purchase. The duplicate comes back.
  • A purchase was made on your account that you did not make — tell us and we will investigate and refund it.
  • The feature was removed or made materially worse after you paid for a permanent unlock, without an equivalent replacement.
  • Credits were taken and the feature did not run, through no fault of yours.
  • We made a material change to prices or terms and you had no reasonable chance to notice before it applied to an ongoing arrangement.

We will not refund credits you have already spent using a feature that worked, on the basis that you have simply changed your mind — unless the law gives you that right. We recognise that is a commercial decision and that it may not be legally effective in your country; see section 5.

5. Your legal rights, in plain terms

This section exists because a "no refunds" line is not the end of the matter.

  • If the app or a feature is faulty, or not of a reasonable standard, the law gives you remedies including a repair, a replacement, a reduction in price, or a refund. That right cannot be taken away by anything on this page.
  • If you bought digital content you have begun to use, the usual 14-day cancellation right may be reduced or removed in some cases — but only because you have had the benefit of what you bought, and only to the extent the law allows.
  • Unfair terms are unenforceable. Under UK consumer law, terms that are so unbalanced in our favour that they would not be accepted in a court of fairness do not have to be honoured, whatever they say. We have not written these terms to be that.
  • Nothing here stops you complaining, or asking a regulator or a court to look at how we have behaved.
If you are a consumer in the UK or EU, you can complain to your national consumer protection body if we do not resolve it — Citizens Advice or Trading Standards in the UK, your national equivalent elsewhere. We would rather you complained to us first, but you are not required to.

6. Chargebacks and fraud

  • If a purchase is charged back, reversed, or found to be fraudulent, the credits, coins or access it granted will be removed.
  • This applies even where the chargeback was not your fault. It is the cost of an honest system: otherwise fraudulent purchases would be cheaper than real ones.
  • Repeatedly disputing genuine charges is account abuse and can lead to suspension.

7. If we do not resolve it

  1. Email vaylosportssupport@gmail.com with the details listed in section 1.
  2. We aim to acknowledge within 30 days, which is the commitment also given for data requests in the Privacy Policy.
  3. If we cannot agree, you can escalate to Google Play for the charge, to your national consumer protection body, or to a court. None of these requires our agreement first.

8. What we have not yet decided

We would rather publish the open question than hide it:

  • Goodwill refunds for unspent credits are a business decision that has not been taken. We have not published a policy because we have not decided one. Until we do, ask us and we will deal with it case by case rather than pretend a rule exists.
  • There is no automatic refund for a feature that is merely not useful to you. The distinction between "not useful" and "faulty" is real and legal, and we will apply it fairly.
  • No refund timescale is committed because refunds are paid by Google, not by us, once a transaction has settled.

If any of this is unclear before you buy, ask us first. A question before purchase is easier than a dispute after.
