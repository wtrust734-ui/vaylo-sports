Contact & Support

Version 1.1 · 8 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports

Email us: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

One inbox handles everything — support, privacy requests, refunds, complaints, copyright notices and child safety reports. There is no ticketing system behind it, so you will be talking to a person.

2. What to include in an email

So we can find your account without asking you to prove everything, include:

  • the email address you signed up with, or your display name;
  • what you want, in one sentence if you can;
  • your device and app version, for anything that is going wrong;
  • a screenshot for any error. It saves a whole round trip.
We will not ask for your password, and you should never send it. We have no need for it and we would treat the message as a phishing attempt. The same goes for full card details — we never see those, because Google handles the payment.

4. Urgent and safety issues

Our inbox is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the police or the emergency number for your country. Do not wait for an email reply.

For child protection in the UK, contact the NSPCC Child Protection Helpline on 0808 8000 111, or Childline on 0800 1111 for an under-18 athlete who needs to talk. Email us as well so we can act on the account, but do not treat that as the route to urgent help.

If AI output in the app led you to harm yourself or someone else, tell us — we want to know, and it will not be treated as an attempt to blame you. See the AI & Coaching Disclaimer.

5. Complaints

  1. Email us with what went wrong. We would rather hear it directly.
  2. We will acknowledge within 30 days — the same commitment we give for data rights requests.
  3. If we cannot resolve it, you can escalate. For a purchase, to Google Play. For consumer law in the UK, to Citizens Advice or Trading Standards. For data protection in the UK, to the Information Commissioner's Office. For data protection elsewhere in the EU, to your local authority.

You do not need our permission to escalate, and nothing in our terms asks you to try us first. We ask because we would rather be able to fix it.

6. Formal legal notices

Legal notices, court documents and regulator correspondence should be sent to vaylosportssupport@gmail.com marked "Formal legal notice" so they are not mistaken for support requests.

The gap. Vaylo Sports has no registered postal address for the service of legal documents at this time, because the registered entity and its address have not been confirmed. Until that is settled, email is the only route — and we accept email as the delivery route rather than pretending a postal address exists. This will be corrected as soon as the registered details are available, and it will be the first thing updated in every document on this site.

7. What we have not committed to

Vaylo Sports is a small operation. We think you should know what that means in practice rather than find out:

  • There is no published response-time target for ordinary support and no 24-hour service. We have committed to 30 days only where we are legally required to.
  • No phone number and no live chat. Email is the route, and it reaches a person.
  • No published support hours, because there are none — messages are read, but not on a rota.
  • Reports are read by people, not automatically triaged. That is deliberate for hard cases and genuinely slower for trivial ones.
  • Moderation staffing, named moderators and a moderation service level have not been decided. The Community Guidelines say so in the same place they list what we do do.

If you have waited a long time for a reply, that is a fair complaint and you are entitled to make it. Email us and say so.
