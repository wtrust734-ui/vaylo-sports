Advertising & Tracking Disclosure

Version 1.2 · 7 October 2026

Operated by Vaylo Sports · Contact: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com

The short version. Vaylo Sports contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracking, and no advertising identifier. We do not read your device advertising ID, and we do not build a profile of you for advertising purposes. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. The only advertising in the app is a small number of sponsored placements, shown to adults only, always labelled, which report back to brands as anonymous counts.

1. What advertising Vaylo actually has

There are four sponsored placements in the app. A placement is a card supplied by a paying brand. What a placement can contain is controlled by us, and every one of them:

  • carries a visible label telling you it is sponsored;
  • is never shown to anyone under 18 — and this is enforced by a database constraint on the placement's minimum age, not just hidden by the interface, so a mistake in a screen cannot expose an under-18 athlete to an advert;
  • reports back nothing about you.

2. What a brand can and cannot see

When a sponsor buys a campaign, they receive reporting for that campaign. Reporting consists of counts, broken down per campaign and per day:

  • how many times the placement was shown;
  • how many times it was tapped.

A brand never receives:

Not shared with brands
Your name, display name or profile photo
Your email address or any contact details
Your location — including anything derived from GPS
Your training, workout, performance or health data
Your sport, age or date of birth
Your user ID, device ID, or advertising ID
Anything that would let a brand identify you or single you out

Athletes under 18 are excluded from this reporting entirely. Their activity is not counted in the numbers a brand receives at all.

3. What is not in the app

To be specific, and because "we don't track you" is only useful if you know what would otherwise be there:

  • No advertising SDKs. There is no Google Mobile Ads, no AppLovin, no Unity Ads, no Meta Audience Network, and no equivalent.
  • No analytics SDKs. No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog, Sentry or equivalent is embedded in the app.
  • No third-party trackers of any kind — no pixels, no tracking pixels in email, no fingerprinting, no session recording.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app does not request or read Google's Advertising ID.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no data broker, data enrichment or people-search provider is involved.
  • No dark patterns. We do not use notification permission prompts as advertising leverage, and we do not make the close button harder to find.

4. In-app promotional messages

The app shows its own messages from time to time — for example an offer to return after a break, or a prompt about a feature you have not used. These are produced by Vaylo Sports, not bought by anyone, they are not third-party advertising, and no third party receives any data about them.

If you dismiss one, the app remembers that on your device so it does not ask again for a while. See the Cookie Policy for how that is stored.

6. Health data is never used for advertising

This is a rule we apply deliberately rather than a feature we have switched off: your training, wellness and health data is not used to select, personalise, target or measure advertising, and is not shared with sponsors. If we ever wanted to do that, it would be a significant change, and you would be told before it happened — not after.

7. If this ever changes

If Vaylo Sports ever introduces advertising or analytics technology that tracks you, we will:

  • update this page and tell you in the app before it applies;
  • ask for consent where the law requires it, and honour a refusal;
  • make it possible to withdraw that consent;
  • update the Google Play Data Safety form to match, which is a legal obligation we cannot avoid.

Queries about advertising or privacy: vaylosportssupport@gmail.com. See also the Privacy Policy.

8. Brand and sponsorship enquiries

This section is the terms for the sponsorship questionnaire on the Vaylo Sports website. It exists as its own numbered section rather than as a separate document because it governs the same relationship as everything above it: what a brand may do with Vaylo, and what Vaylo will not do for a brand. (The questionnaire itself is at the Sponsors address on the website’s own menu. It is not linked from here because this document is also read inside the app, where that address does not exist — a link that works in one place and 404s in the other is worse than a sentence.)

8.1 It is an enquiry, not an order

Sending the questionnaire does not buy anything, reserve anything, create a contract, or start a campaign. There is no price on it, nothing is charged, and nothing you select appears in the app as a result. A deal becomes real only when a person at Vaylo Sports has agreed it with you in writing and somebody has set it up by hand. Until then you are owed nothing and neither are we.

8.2 What the questionnaire collects

When you submit it, we store:

  • the brand or company name, and the website if you give one;
  • the name and email address of the person making the enquiry, so a person can reply;
  • the options you selected, including the interests you were asked about and did not select, so a reply does not re-ask what you have already declined;
  • the sports and regions you entered, and anything you wrote in the free-text box;
  • the version of these terms you accepted and the date and time you accepted it;
  • a short, salted, one-way hash of the network address the submission came from, used only to rate-limit submissions and investigate abuse. It is not stored in a readable form and is not used to identify you.

It is used to reply to you and to decide whether there is a deal worth discussing. It is not sold, not rented, and not shared with anyone. It is kept while the enquiry is live and for a reasonable period afterwards for our own records; you can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to vaylosportssupport@gmail.com and we will. The general rules in the Privacy Policy and your rights under the Your Data Rights document apply to it in full, including the right to complain to a regulator.

Enquiries are for business contacts aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly accept a sponsorship enquiry from anyone under 18, and we do not use the questionnaire to collect anything about athletes.

8.3 Brand preference never changes what an athlete is told

A brand may not buy influence over the training, recovery, load, readiness or injury-risk output an athlete sees, over a plan they are given, or over anything an AI feature writes to them. This is not a policy we can be persuaded out of by a larger budget, and it is not a negotiating position: an athlete has to be able to trust that the guidance about their own body was written for them.

The questionnaire asks brands about an “AI preference toward your brand” because brands ask us for it. It is recorded as an expression of interest and marked not implemented in the questionnaire itself. The underlying capability does not exist: rows can be stored and nothing reads them. Nothing about a brand influences what an athlete is told today, and nothing about a brand will influence it without this page saying so first, in plain English, before it applies.

8.4 What a brand has to promise back

  • The label stays. Every sponsored placement carries a disclosure label. A deal that needs the label removed is not a deal we can do.
  • No appeal to under-18s. Sponsored content is hidden from anyone without an adult date of birth, enforced in the database. A creative or an offer aimed at minors will be refused, and the limitation that age is self-declared is stated plainly here rather than glossed over.
  • No athlete-level data, ever. Reporting is aggregate impression and click counts with no user column. No brand receives a name, a location, training data or health data, in any form, including a form described as anonymised.
  • No health claims in a placement. A sponsored card is not medical or performance advice and may not be written to look like it.

Anything a brand would need that is not on that list is a question for a person, not a form. Write to vaylosportssupport@gmail.com.
