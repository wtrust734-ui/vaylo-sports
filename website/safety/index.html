Safety Centre

What Vaylo can do, what it cannot, and how to tell us when it goes wrong

Everything on this page is also inside the app, under Profile → Legal & Safety Centre. Nothing here is a substitute for a doctor, a physiotherapist, a coach or a parent.

AI safety

Parts of Vaylo write text using a large language model. A language model predicts plausible sentences rather than understanding your body, which means it can be fluent, specific and wrong at the same time.

  • It is confidently wrong, not obviously wrong. It rarely says “I don’t know”.
  • It cannot see you. It knows what you logged, not how you slept or what happened today.
  • It does not know your medical history, your medication, an injury you never mentioned, or a condition you have not been diagnosed with.
  • VPR, training load and recovery are not AI. They are arithmetic on your own entries, and we would rather you trusted those.

Nutrition features are blocked for under-18s, Injury Management never diagnoses, and the app carries a permanent notice telling you to see a professional. Read the full disclaimer.

Training and coaching safety

Vaylo caps suggested training volume by age band, and gives young athletes a deload week more often. For a load above a safe threshold, it raises an overtraining alert against your own data.

A cap is not a plan. It stops the app suggesting an unreasonable week. It does not know you are growing, that a growth spurt is coming, that you have a condition, or that you play three sports. Use the cap as a ceiling, not a target.

  • Stop and see a professional if you have sharp, worsening or persistent pain, are returning from injury, are pregnant, or take medication that affects what is safe.
  • Challenges and packs are not prescriptions. They describe a structure, not a plan for you.
  • Your coach’s plan beats our numbers. If the app disagrees with your coach, follow your coach.

Training & health safety notice

Health-related limitations

  • No diagnosis, ever. Nothing in the app identifies an injury or a condition.
  • No treatment plan. Injury Management organises a rehab a professional has already prescribed.
  • Not medical advice. If it contradicts what a doctor or physiotherapist has told you, they are right.
  • Not for eating disorders. Calorie and weight features are blocked under 18, and we ask you not to use them if the app’s output makes you feel worse.

If AI output led you to harm yourself or someone else, tell us. It will not be used against you. Report it.

Young athlete safety

For athletes under 18, the following are switched off. Each is enforced in the database or checked before the feature runs, so a bug in a screen cannot expose a young athlete to it.

No advertising

Suppressed entirely, and young athletes are excluded from sponsor reporting.

No nutrition advice

Calorie and weight features blocked with an explanation.

Private by default

A minor’s profile cannot be made public at all.

Cannot buy

Credits, packs and subscriptions are not available to under-18 accounts.

Full young athlete policy

Child safety

Vaylo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. We apply the UK Children’s Code and age-appropriate design principles to the version aimed at 13–17 year olds: data minimisation, privacy by default, no behavioural advertising, no profiling.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police or the NSPCC Child Protection Helpline on 0808 8000 111. Do not wait for us, and do not treat a support form as a route to urgent help.

  • Content sexualising a child is removed immediately, and reported to the authorities where the law permits or requires it.
  • An adult contacting a young athlete is treated as harassment: content removed, account actioned.
  • A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child’s account at any time, with no reason required, and we do it.

Read the full policy, including what we have not finalised

Community safety

The feed, communities, challenges and leaderboards are the parts of Vaylo other people can see. The rules are short:

  • No bullying, harassment or threats — including pile-ons.
  • No racist, sexist, homophobic or transphobic content.
  • No sexual content, and nothing sexualising a child.
  • No encouraging self-harm, disordered eating or dangerous training.
  • No posting someone else’s information without their consent.
  • No impersonating Vaylo, our staff, a coach or another athlete.
  • No unsolicited selling in the community.

Profiles are private by default, and you control what leaves your account. Full community guidelines

Reporting

Report something, or block someone

Content inside the app: use the Report control on any shared activity or profile. It reaches a person with the context attached, which is faster and more useful than any form on this page.

Blocking: available from any profile. Blocking removes their shared activity from your feed in both directions and stops them sending you friend requests. They are not told they were blocked. Blocking does not remove their content — report it as well if you want it gone.

Something else, or you cannot get into the app: use the form below.

Report a safety concern

Every message is read by a person. Pick the category that fits closest; if it is a child safety concern, choose that one and read the notice below the form.

Choosing the right one gets your message to a person faster.
We need this to reply to you. It is not added to any marketing list, because we have no marketing list.
The email on the account, if the concern is about a specific athlete or account.
What happened, what you expected, and anything you have already tried.

What you send is used to answer you and for nothing else. It is not added to a mailing list and it is not sold or shared. You can ask us to delete it at any time — see the Privacy & Data Centre.

If a child is at risk, do not rely on this form.

  • Immediate danger — call the police.
  • UK Child Protection Helpline (NSPCC) — 0808 8000 111.
  • A young person who needs to talk — Childline, 0800 1111.

Send the form as well so we can act on the account, but do not wait for our reply before getting help.

What happens after you report

  • A person reviews it. Reports are not triaged by an automated filter.
  • Only you can see that you reported. The athlete does not.
  • Reporting twice does not stack — the app tells you it is already reported.
  • Enforcement is proportionate: remove content, warn, suspend, or close the account.

We have not published a response-time target, because we have not committed to one. If you wait a long time, that is a fair complaint. See the enforcement steps.
