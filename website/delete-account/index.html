Account

Delete your Vaylo Sports account

You can do this yourself, in the app, without asking us and without giving a reason. It is immediate and it is not reversible.

Fastest route: in the app, go to Profile → Delete account permanently. It re-asks you to confirm, then deletes and verifies. No email, no waiting, no approval.

Self-service

Deleting in the app

  1. Open the app, sign in, and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account permanently, which sits under Sign Out. The same screen is reachable from Profile → Legal & Safety → Delete your account.
  3. Confirm it is you. Enter your password and tap Verify. If you do not sign in with a password, tap Re-authenticate and sign in with the provider you normally use. This is deliberate: deletion asks for a fresh sign-in so a session left open on a shared device cannot destroy somebody else’s account.
  4. Type DELETE MY ACCOUNT and tick the acknowledgement box.
  5. Tap Delete Account, then Permanently Delete. The app runs the deletion and checks afterwards that nothing remains in any table that can hold your data.

What you see afterwards is a verified result, not a promise. The app counts rows in every table that can hold your data and tells you what it found. If anything survived, it says so rather than claiming success.

From the web

Requesting deletion without the app

If you cannot get into the app — no longer have access to the email, or you would simply rather not use the app — you can ask us to delete the account. This is a request, and we will confirm when it is done. It is not instant, because we have to verify that you are the account owner before destroying anything.

  • Include the email address the Vaylo account was created with. Without it we cannot find the account, and we will not guess.
  • We will reply to that address to confirm who we think you are before deleting anything.
  • If you cannot access that address, tell us what else you can prove — username, approximate sign-up date — and we will work through it with you.
  • Download your data first if you want a copy. Deletion cannot be undone.

The full process, including verification and everything that is removed, is in the Account Deletion notice.

What happens

What deletion removes

  • Your profile, avatar and account settings
  • Every training session, measurement and derived metric
  • Health and wellness data you connected or entered
  • Everything you posted: shared activities, notes, images and videos
  • Communities, challenges and leaderboard entries
  • Messages and friend connections
  • Purchase and entitlement records linked to your user id
  • Uploaded files, including form-analysis video
  • Reports you filed about other content
  • The sign-in itself, so the login can no longer be used

What survives, and why

One anonymous record. A salted hash of your user id, a status and a timestamp. No name, no email, nothing that identifies you, and nothing from which your account can be reconstructed.

Purchase and tax records. Kept as long as accounting law requires, separate from your profile and unlinked to your user id. This is a legal obligation, not a choice.

Nothing else. If you think something of yours survives that is not on this list, tell us and we will check it.

Before you go

Three things worth doing first

  1. Cancel any subscription in Google Play. Deleting your Vaylo account does not cancel a Google Play subscription, and an app you have deleted is not somewhere you can cancel from. Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
  2. Download your data. Profile → Legal & Safety Centre → Download your data. It takes seconds and it cannot be done afterwards.
  3. Note down anything you will miss. Unspent credits and coins are lost with the account. Some things — an injury history, the notes on a rehab — are genuinely hard to recreate.

Deletion cannot be undone. There is no recovery window and no support ticket that can bring the account back. Make sure you have what you need first.

Deleting only some of your data

You do not have to delete everything to stop sharing it. Disconnecting Health Connect or a wearable stops that data being used, and privacy settings control what leaves your account.

Privacy & data centre

Something is wrong instead

If you want an account deleted because of something that happened on Vaylo — harassment, a breach, a safety failure — please say so in the message. Those are not routine requests and we do not treat them as routine.
