Features

Everything Vaylo Sports does, and what state it is in

Each area below is labelled with whether it works right now, is built but not yet switched on, or is deliberately turned off. We publish the last two because the alternative is a feature page describing something you cannot use.

Sports supported: Running, Cycling, Swimming, Football, Basketball, Tennis, Rugby, Athletics, Triathlon, Boxing, MMA, Cricket, Gym / Weightlifting, CrossFit, Climbing, Rowing, Volleyball, Hockey, Badminton, Gymnastics, Wrestling, Golf, Combat Sports, Martial Arts, Other.

This is the list the app offers. If your sport is not on it, choose Other and log your sessions anyway — the core logging and metrics do not depend on the sport.

Train and log

Working today

Performance Log

Log sessions with RPE, duration, notes and form. It is the record everything else is calculated from.

Training plans

Week-by-week plans built from what you have logged, your sport and your availability.

Routines

Your own repeatable sessions, with history, so a good week is not rebuilt from memory.

Goals and event preparation

Set goals, track them against your log, and work towards a specific event.

Understand your training

Working today

Performance Dashboard

Readiness, acute load, A:C ratio, calories, protein and an injury-risk flag, calculated from your own entries.

VPR performance rating

A single rating built from the metrics you log. Arithmetic on your data — not a prediction and not AI.

Limiter Test

Find which limiter is holding you back, then work on that one rather than guessing.

Development trajectory

A trend line across your own VPR history. It describes what happened; it does not forecast what will.

Equipment Tracker

Log the gear you train on and rotate it before it becomes the problem.

Recovery, injury and the body

Working today

Recovery tracking

Sleep, soreness, mood and energy, and how they read next to your training load.

Injury Management

A generic return-to-play scaffold to organise a rehab you have already been given. It does not diagnose, and it carries a permanent notice telling you to see a physiotherapist or doctor.

Nutrition

Calorie and protein tracking against your training. Restricted to athletes aged 18 and over.

Form analysis

Upload video of a lift or movement and review it frame by frame against your own earlier sets.

Skill and tactics

Working today

Learning Hub

Written lessons, drills and quizzes across sports, including load management for growing athletes.

Tactical analysis

Organise opponents, read matchups and keep notes on what worked.

Cross-training

Work out what a second sport is doing for your first one, and where the two are pulling against each other.

Compare

Put two periods side by side and see what actually changed.

Motivation and progress

Working today

Mental Gym

Work on the mental side of competing — pre-performance routines, focus and managing a bad day.

Personal bests and streaks

Record your bests, keep a streak, and buy a shield for the day you miss.

Achievements and trophies

Milestones worth collecting as you build consistency.

Event Packs

Structured programmes for a specific event, from beginner to elite, with taper and race-day guidance.

Arcade

A short arcade break built around your training, for the days when the training is done.

The community side

Working today

Feed

Share a session, achievement, goal or personal best — and see what other athletes choose to share. Private by default.

Communities and challenges

Group by sport, and take on a challenge alongside other athletes.

Leaderboard and friends

See where you sit, follow the athletes you want to train alongside.

Report and block

Report harmful content from any post or profile, and block any athlete. Blocking is not disclosed to them.

AI features

Built, not yet switched on

These are built into the app and appear in its navigation, but the server capability that runs them is not switched on yet. The app tells the athlete so when they open the feature, rather than showing a broken result. We would rather say this than advertise something that does not answer.

Vaylo Coach chat

Free-text conversation about your training, with generated replies.

Generated training weeks

A plan written for your current week rather than a template.

Weekly coach review

Generated feedback on your goals and your consistency.

Video form analysis

Generated commentary on a video you upload.

Calorie scan and nutrition plans

Generated eating plans. Restricted to athletes aged 18 and over.

Tactical briefings

Generated match-up notes from your own description.

Not switched on

Deliberately off

Deliberately disabled, with the reason recorded in the code. They may change; nothing below is promised.

Wearable and health sync

Strava, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura, WHOOP, Polar, Suunto, COROS and Android Health Connect. The integration needs signed developer agreements with each provider before it can exchange a token, so no data moves.

GPS tracking

Live distance during a workout. The Android build does not request location permission at all, so it is inert by design rather than by oversight.

Coach marketplace

Buying coaching. The surface is built but ships with no coaches, and an empty marketplace is worse than no marketplace.
